BioSimLab

A virtual science laboratory where students practise real lab protocols before they touch the bench.

RoleUI/UX design
PlatformWeb app
IndustryEdTech and life sciences
ToolsFigma
01

Overview

BioSimLab is an AI-powered virtual science laboratory for bioscience students, graduates and researchers. Learners practise core protocols, starting with DNA extraction, through guided simulation before they use physical equipment, and instructors can see who is ready.

02

Project objective

  • Give students unlimited, low-cost practice of lab protocols before they use real equipment.
  • Make every step clear: what to do, in which order and why.
  • Give instant, specific feedback instead of generic errors.
  • Keep learners motivated with rewards and visible progress.
  • Support both students and instructors, starting at sign-up.
03

Scope

  • Landing page that explains the problem and the solution
  • Sign-up for students and instructors
  • Simulation workspace: protocol steps, lab bench, tasks and lab notebook
  • Rewards: BioCoins and achievement badges
04

Key design decisions

A landing page that leads with the problem

The page opens with a plain promise, "The bench before the bench", a preview of a gel result and a clear Launch Free Lab button. It then sets out the problem, the solution and how a student uses it.

Sign-up that adapts to the role

Students and instructors pick their role with a toggle, and the form changes to ask the right questions for each, next to a short testimonial panel.

A protocol rail that shows progress

Eight steps in two phases, each with its timing and a current, done or locked state, so learners always know where they are and what comes next.

A lab bench you can see

Pipettes, tips, buffers, centrifuge, heat block, vortex and ice are drawn and labelled on a bench, while the science panel above explains why each step matters.

Tasks that must be done in order

Each step lists tasks to complete in sequence, with a clear warning that out-of-order actions fail the experiment. The next-step button stays locked until the tasks are done.

Rewards that make practice stick

A BioCoins balance, achievement badges and a history of earned coins turn repeated practice into progress, while the Lab Notebook keeps a session log beside the work.

Case studyUI/UX design

34NG Scale-Up Admin

An admin console for a merit-based cooperative: members, contributions, funding and equipment in one place.

RoleUI/UX design
PlatformWeb admin dashboard
IndustryFintech and cooperative funding
ToolsFigma
01

Overview

34NG Scale-Up is a cooperative platform built on the idea "Grow together. Earn by merit. Get funded." Members contribute, earn merit points and apply for funding to get equipment. The admin console lets a super admin manage members, cells and zones, track contributions and wallets, review funding applications, oversee vendors and assets, and set the rules that run the platform.

02

Project objective

  • Give admins a clear view of members, contributions and funding at a glance.
  • Move funding applications through a visible pipeline, from submission to equipment delivery.
  • Keep the cooperative transparent, with the pool and its spending shown openly.
  • Catch risk early with a fraud and verification center.
  • Let admins configure points, funding and delivery rules without code.
03

Scope

  • Dashboard with KPIs, trends and funding pipeline
  • Members and cells management
  • General settings and organization profile
  • Contribution settings
  • Points system: rules and a condition builder
  • Funding rules: funding tiers and disbursement settings
04

Key design decisions

A dashboard that answers "how are we doing?"

Four KPI cards, contribution trends and a funding pipeline put the health of the cooperative on one screen, with comparisons to last month.

A funding pipeline you can read at a glance

Applications move through five colour-coded stages: Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Procurement and Delivered, each with a count and the latest application IDs.

Risk and quick actions up front

A Risk & Fraud Center flags suspicious accounts, failed verifications, missed check-ins and duplicate NIN or BVN attempts. Common tasks sit one click away.

Management screens built for scale

Summary cards, filters, bulk actions and a dense table with status and performance chips make it practical to manage tens of thousands of cells and members.

Rules admins can configure without code

Merit point rules are a sortable table with toggles, point values and frequencies, backed by caps, limits, a live example calculation and a priority chain.

Delivery rules in clear groups

Delivery configuration, requirements and verification steps are grouped into clear blocks, with a summary panel so admins can check the policy at a glance.

05

A web marketplace that brings Kantin Kwari's fabric and fashion trade online for buyers.

RoleUI/UX design
PlatformWeb, buyer side
IndustryE-commerce: textiles and fashion
ToolsFigma
01

Overview

Kwari E-Market is the digital extension of Kantin Kwari Market in Kano, a major textile hub. The buyer website lets people browse fabrics, shoes and fashion from verified vendors, buy by the yard or as a full bill of bundles, pay, track their orders and manage their account.

02

Project objective

  • Move a traditional textile market online in a way buyers already understand.
  • Make browsing fast with categories, trending items, new arrivals and best sellers.
  • Support how fabric is really bought: by the yard, by the bundle or as a full bill.

03

Scope

Buy a full bill or individual bundles

Orders you can scan and track

Status tabs with counts, colour-coded status chips, item thumbnails, total and payment method, plus Buy Again and Track Order actions on each order.
